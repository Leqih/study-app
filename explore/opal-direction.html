Why this one can ship

One hero per screenThe dessert, with a soft glow behind it. Nothing else competes: no stickers, badges, tilted headings or decorative type.
One accentStrawberry pink, used only as the glow, the progress fill, the eyebrow and the ring. Buttons are white or grey; cards are one grey with a hairline edge.
Clear hierarchyTwo weights only: 700 for titles and numbers, 400–600 for everything else. Secondary text is the same white at 60% and 32%.
Fewer modulesHome has four things: today's bake, two numbers, the week, friends. Mood picks, search and shop content move to their own tabs.
Native partsStandard tab bar, lists, capsule buttons, a ring gauge. All of it maps to SwiftUI defaults; dark mode is the default, so there's one theme to build.
What it needsDesserts redrawn once in a soft, lit style (one illustrator, one set) so they hold up as the only hero. The current flat art works as a stand-in.
